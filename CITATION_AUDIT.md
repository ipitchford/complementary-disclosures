## Closest comparisons actually inspected

**Chen and Waggoner, *Informational substitutes*, arXiv:1703.08636v1, Section 5.1.** The independent-bit XOR example already gives arbitrary-order abstract complementarity. The network constructions must be positioned as constrained clearing realisations and quantitative characterisations, not discovery of high-order complementarity. Their framework concerns expected decision utility; prior-weighted full-accounting-fibre width is a different objective. https://arxiv.org/html/1703.08636v1

**Visentin, Battiston and D'Errico, *Rethinking financial contagion*, arXiv:1608.07831v1, Section 3.2, Equation 9.** Loss conservation allocates the initial external-asset loss between bank equity and external creditors. Parent also read Supplement SI.2.2, Proposition 6 and its proof. The candidate's loss–shock inequality is a specialised consequence, not an unqualified new theorem. https://arxiv.org/html/1608.07831v1

**Ahn, Chen and Kim, *Robust risk quantification via shock propagation in financial networks*, DOI 10.1287/opre.2020.0722.** Read the author-hosted full-text Section 4.2, especially 4.2.1–4.2.6. This is close antecedent work, not merely generic context: it formulates worst-case default probabilities with information from individual or multiple banks, large exposures, link existence and aggregate information, and uses an MILP framework with some simpler cases. Section 4.2.3 explicitly discusses exposures at least ten percent of bank capital; 4.2.4 models observed nonzero links with a positive lower bound; 4.2.5 treats aggregate-only information. The candidate instead fixes a shock, measures the diameter of possible outside-creditor losses inside report fibres, and compares selectable finite menus. Those distinctions do not license a claim that information-sensitive robust network optimisation itself is new. The article's Theorems 2–3 concern asymptotic default-probability comparisons under specified random-shock assumptions; they are not the candidate's finite-fibre width theorems. https://www1.se.cuhk.edu.hk/~nchenweb/Publications/ahn-et-al-2023-robust-risk-quantification-via-shock-propagation-in-financial-networks.pdf

**Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization* (2004), Sections 5.2.3–5.2.4, pp. 226–227.** Read the refined affine-constraint qualification and LP strong-duality/attainment statements in the author-hosted text. The affine-simplex menu formulas and their bounded-error dual are explicit applications of standard finite LP duality. The contribution is their diagnostic application and certificate representation, not a new duality theorem or general new LP algorithm. https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf
