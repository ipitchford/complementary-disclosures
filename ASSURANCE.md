## Review disposition and limits

The revision addresses the zero-factor error in Theorem B, incorporates the reviewer's one-interbank-layer affine example, distinguishes full feasible fibres from posterior support, and separates restricted report menus from unrestricted information. It extends the product-noise calculation to heterogeneous errors over the whole nonnegative domain and specializes standard LP duality to affine simplex menus. The latter is not a new duality theorem or a polynomial-time solution of arbitrary report-subset selection.

Computational repairs separate rational witnesses from algebraic models, retain query histories, distinguish requested from actual edge counts, and label the selection target as half the initial upper bracket rather than half the unknown exact width. A small bracket containing zero does not prove zero width. See `revision-response.md` for item-level disposition. Completion of execution includes unresolved results, not successful solution of every instance.

The companion note's clearing model, pair formulation, witness-cut framework and initial constructions are inherited and consolidated, not newly discovered here. Abstract informational complementarity, loss conservation, optimal recovery and LP duality have prior art. The defensible contribution is the scoped clearing constructions and exact menu/prior/precision analysis. A specialist contribution is plausible; no four-star outcome follows merely from correctness or extension count. The synthetic study does not estimate prevalence in real networks or establish supervisory scalability, and solver timing comparisons do not isolate a causal effect of cycles.
