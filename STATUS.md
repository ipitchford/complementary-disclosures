# Status and claim boundary

Version 2.0.0-candidate revises and consolidates the supplied 13 October 2026 manuscript and its unpublished companion. Archive availability is established by the public provider records, not this scientific status file.

The subject is a mathematical identification problem in one-period proportional clearing, not an empirical assessment of any institution. Reports are truthful observations; they do not change behaviour, contracts or shocks. Positive outside debt, fixed known balance-sheet totals, a specified report menu and the absence of bankruptcy costs are material assumptions.

The supplied review identified a false boundary case in the prior-dependent submodularity theorem and a distinction missing from the explanation: accounting constraints and report-menu restrictions can generate complementarity even with affine loss. The revision addresses these directly. A solver-only satisfiability answer must not be represented as an independently checked rational witness.

Written proofs, finite exact tests, solver answers, producer-side replay, supplied review and formal verification are distinct kinds of evidence. No external peer-review acceptance, formal verification, empirical validation or official REF rating is claimed.
